# Minimalist Product Ad

Create a premium minimalist product short from the supplied product media and facts.

## Product fidelity

- Preserve the exact product category, silhouette, body color, accent color, material tint, finish, controls, ports, packaging, and variant relationships visible in the references. Minimalist styling may change background, light, and composition, but never recolor or redesign the product unless explicitly requested.
- Use one main variant. Supporting variants may enter later as rhythm or a final family composition; avoid grids, product walls, scattered ecommerce layouts, and showing every variant at once.

## Narrative and motion

- Select a compact spine: Product Launch = negative-space reveal → hero view → material/structure detail → concrete product action → payoff → final copy; Feature Touch = stillness → interaction trigger → mechanism → detail → result → close; Color Family = hero variant → supporting variants → ordered relation → unified finish.
- The opening must immediately reveal an attractive product angle or physically plausible action, not empty waiting time.
- Drive transitions with product edges, moving highlights, rotation, opening, snapping, sliding, folding, screen changes, matched silhouettes, or variant arrangement. Use one main action per beat and delay secondary elements.
- Preserve clean negative space and readable silhouette. Avoid mirrored white floors, cheap glass stages, random particles, fake technology UI, meaningless flashes, and generic motion unrelated to the product.

## Typography

- Use supplied copy when available. Never invent product claims. If copy is requested but absent, use neutral sensory wording rather than a factual claim.
- Prefer a single short line, approximately 3–5 words, with no wrapping, title/subtitle stack, lower-third subtitle placement, or multiple text blocks.
- Integrate text into the composition with restrained same-line fade or slide motion. Keep it stable long enough to read and use at most two text colors derived from the scene and actual product color.

## Camera and audio

- Use controlled macro detail, slow arcs, precise push-ins, and restrained focus shifts. Material highlights must reveal real shape and finish.
- Use sparse product sounds and refined music with clear braking moments; avoid exaggerated impacts that make the product feel cheap.
